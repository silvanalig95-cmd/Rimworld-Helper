# Rimfolk changelog

What changed in each update of Rimfolk, newest first, written for players. The same list is the "What's new" page at
the top of the Almanac in the game, and a letter shows the newest round once when you load a save after an update.

## Answers that happen before they're told

*18 October 2026, version 0.6.1*

- An answer that sets something going on the map no longer tells its outcome the moment it's given. It says when it's planned ("The harvest dance is planned for this evening"), and the story is told once it has happened, with how it went (meals eaten, jobs done). If it falls through, you hear that instead.
- Answers that describe something done now do it on the map in 38 more places: the thief is punished in front of everyone at supper, the doctor sits at the bedside with the last dose, the snow is shovelled, the bees are smoked out, and the tribute, the bounty, the envoy's price and medicine for sick neighbours are carried out to the gate by hand.

## Building up a defense, and the land's year

*17 October 2026, night, version 0.6.0*

- Defense lines: pick a gap on Colony → Defenses and build it up step by step with the helper, from closing the gap and clearing the field of fire, through cover, traps and guns, to a killbox, a second layer of wall and a line to fall back to. Steps tick themselves off from what stands, the line's aim (a gate, a killbox, a turret line, an archers' wall) orders them, and "Plan this step" opens the plan already placed and turned for the line. The killbox step says whether it fits behind the gap, what has to be dug or taken down, or where it would fit instead.
- The helper reviews each line: where raiders could walk round it ("the wall stops 4 cells short of the rock to the west", with a button to wall it off), who sees the gate and from how far, trees and chunks raiders would hide behind (cut and haul in one click), turret spacing, power and fuel, wood in the wall, and a rough strength against today's raids.
- Lines learn from raids: who came through the gate, went round or broke through, and where colonists fell. The lesson brings the right step to the front, and a reminder comes once per raid if a line's gate is shut. Defense plans now dig into unexplored rock the way room plans do.
- Four new defense plans for building a line up step by step: a sandbag or barricade line behind a gate, a second layer of wall, a small killbox for narrow gaps and bows, and a tunnel killbox for mountain bases. The killbox is deeper, with its shooters out of grenade reach and walls between turrets and shooters, and the turret nest shields its repairer.
- Five new Almanac pages on defending: defense lines step by step, where a killbox fits, killbox mistakes, the field of fire (weapon reach by era, and darkness as 1.6 really has it) and keeping a defense up. "How a killbox works" is rewritten in real RimWorld terms.
- Wishes follow the land's year: clothes for the coming season (warm, or cool on hot land), measured by what the colonist wears; a freezer in hot country; and on cold land a sauna, with Dubs Bad Hygiene or without, where people sit and talk on cold days. Fishing wishes work with Odyssey or Vanilla Fishing Expanded.
- The planting day: when the fields open after a cold season, the council asks whether to sow together. Held, it's a sowing party and a word at the field's edge in the evening, and a crop sown in full is remembered as a good start. You can also put a planting day on the calendar yourself.
- The animals: a favourite kind of animal (or Pokémon) to wish for, a pack animal of one's own, and bringing the herd home before winter or after a raid, each animal walked into the barn or pen on a rope.

## Fungus meals and digging into the dark

*17 October 2026, evening, version 0.5.1*

- With Ideology, a "cook fungus meal" bill at any stove makes simple meals that always have fungus in them, a kind of meal of their own, so one colonist's food policy can take only these while everyone else's leaves them out (Alpha Memes' fungus diet no longer makes them sick). A new Almanac page, "fungus meals", shows how to set it up.
- Room plans reach into unexplored ground: the inside is marked for mining and dug out as the colonists get there, rock in the walls stays as the wall, and the rest of the room (walls on open ground, the door, furniture and floor) follows as the ground comes to light.

## Defenses, animals and fewer repeats

*17 October 2026, version 0.5.0*

- Colony → Defenses reads your map: the edges raiders come from, the narrow places on their way in, whether the home is walled in, and advice ranked by what matters now, with eleven defense plans you place like room plans. Fifteen new Almanac pages on defending the colony.
- The advisor watches 62 kinds of thing: a mission running out, plague or famine in the region, a camp's question or an office left waiting, the low-tech winter, and rooms that could be better. Some advice takes the first step for you.
- The colony's animals: the herdmaster's rounds, an animal of one's own to wish for, stories of an old companion or an orphan raised by hand, hard choices about the herd, and the lambing watch and shearing day as traditions. With PokéWorld, trainers spar and talk moves, and the council can set out to fill a Pokédex.
- Four new scenarios: The Elders' Fire, What the Ship Carried, The Orphanage (Biotech) and The Land Grant. A colony can become known for its hospitality, its faith or the days it keeps, and people earn names from their years in a seat, the faith and the colony.
- Health that follows what happened: a cough after the flu, swellings after the plague, a limp once a broken leg has healed, bad nights after a fight at home, and with the DLCs a pollution cough, unease after a horror and sickness aloft. The hospital earns a name of its own that eases its patients and draws the sick from the road; convalescence can follow hunger, cold or an illness nobody tended.
- Nemeses in dreams, a story of the fear laid to rest once one is caught, and the day one first came back on the calendar; feelings now move with the camps and the region's troubles.
- About 3,000 new lines where colonists repeated themselves most: everyday journals, wish reminders and thanks, personal touches, meeting talk, moments between colonists, dreams over breakfast, what colonists say aloud, walks, vigils and hunts, camps' requests, the newer decrees, and around the animals (a pet's mischief, the herd's habits, a Pokémon evolving). What two colonists say when one walks over to the other (a secret confided, a mentor's thanks, the physician's rounds, the marshal's word) now goes a dozen or more ways. The lines heard most come round about once a year or less.
- This page, a CHANGELOG.md with the same list, and a letter when you load a save after an update (a new colony gets the welcome letter instead; the Interface page of the settings can turn it off). The council window now reopens on the page you last had open (a setting can make it open on This week instead).

## The colony's year, the world beyond and people's lives

*16 October 2026, afternoon*

- Conversations happen where people are: a colonist walks over before talking, and every line is spoken the same way, as a bubble or floating text.
- Wishes, feasts and toasts respect each colonist's beliefs and diet.
- The colony's year: new decrees and offices, calendar days, customs and council projects for a settled people.
- The world beyond the walls: ten regional situations, eight new kinds of camp with requests of their own, missions of the road, and peoples who remember single colonists.
- People's lives: personal stories of three, life goals that grow up with a child, more for the young and the old.
- More answers play out on the map: work parties, walks, vigils, searches, errands and hunts (only safe game, and nothing someone else has claimed).
- Wall lamps chosen by room size and hung on a real wall; mods round three (Outland, Medieval Total Rim, Alpha Genes and Memes); a lighter pass on the game.

## Setbacks, promises and This week

*16 October 2026, morning*

- The council window opens on This week: what waits on you, what's coming and how people are. Everyone is one sortable table of the colonists, and Dilemmas keeps every question and how each answer went.
- 41 setbacks: bad moods that come from what happens in play, each with a hint about what helps.
- Promises kept: a granted wish can be taken back, and colonists notice.
- Good moods are smaller and fewer; many small good moments now give something else (rest, recreation, a lesson, trust or a feeling).
- Rimfolk's systems react to each other, and colonists say aloud how they feel, talk about the weather and keep small habits (a grave visited, the stars watched, a slow walk).
- A balance pass from a simulated colony year, and your other mods in Rimfolk's newer systems.

## The calendar, personal stories and answers that play out

*15 October 2026*

- The colony calendar: days that matter, kept each year.
- Sixteen personal stories that unfold over seasons, and health stories: convalescence, sickbed visits, doctors' and midwives' records.
- Answers play out on the map: when a council answer says people feast, light a fire, raise a toast or visit a grave, they do.
- What the world's peoples remember of the colony, and events that follow from it.
- A settings window with pages, search and presets; one evening letter for the day's smaller news; room themes and advice when the colony outgrows a room.
- The breadth round: more secrets, vendettas, offices, customs, names, scenarios and feelings, and 1,830 more lines for the thinnest pools.
- A more readable Almanac, with tables and a text size.

## RimWorld 1.7, ailments, and more to say

*14 October 2026*

- Rimfolk runs on RimWorld 1.7 as well as 1.6, with one file for both.
- Everyday ailments (colds, strained backs, sunburn) and a rare tier of serious illnesses.
- Visual and sound touches from the game's own effects: sky tints, fireflies, camp smoke, celebrations.
- About 1,700 new lines, and a memory that keeps a line from coming round again too soon.
- Good times turn ordinary: good memories fade sooner, and standards rise to cover the game's comforts.
- Room plans: 28 room types and 108 layouts. A welcome letter, a "Start here" page and a self-check.
- More wishes, council projects, life goals, dilemmas, encounters, decrees and quirks, and a soft integration for PokéWorld.

## Meetings that happen, and rooms of every size

*13 October 2026*

- Council meetings are announced, colonists are called in, and those who miss one miss it for a reason.
- Rewards are earned instead of handed out, and everyday pressures push back on a colony that has it easy.
- Rooms: sizes and styles, eighteen new rooms, your own saved rooms, and how much of each material you have in the build menu.

## Everyday life and the heart

*12 October 2026*

- Everyday journal entries drawn from what is on a colonist's mind.
- Everyday frictions with each other and with the home itself: sticking doors, leaks, smoke and clutter.
- Feelings and strain: a layer under mood that builds through hard times and eases with rest, friends and quiet.
