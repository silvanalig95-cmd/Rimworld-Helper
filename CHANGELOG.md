# Rimfolk changelog

What changed in each update of Rimfolk, newest first, written for players. The same list is the "What's new" page at
the top of the Almanac in the game, and a letter shows the newest round once when you load a save after an update.

## Fewer repeats, and what's new

*16 October 2026, night, version 0.5.0*

- About 2,700 new lines where colonists repeated themselves most: everyday journal entries, wish reminders and thanks, personal touches, meeting talk, moments between colonists, dreams told over breakfast, what colonists say aloud, and the newer walks, vigils, searches and hunts. The lines heard most now come round about once a year or less.
- Moments between colonists can get more lines from separate files, the same way flavour lines already could.
- This page, a CHANGELOG.md in the mod's folder, and a letter when you load a save after an update. A new colony gets the welcome letter instead.

## The colony's year, the world beyond and people's lives

*16 October 2026, afternoon*

- Conversations happen where people are: a colonist walks over before talking, and every line is spoken the same way, as a bubble or floating text.
- Wishes, feasts and toasts respect each colonist's beliefs and diet.
- The colony's year: new decrees and offices, calendar days, customs and council projects for a settled people.
- The world beyond the walls: ten regional situations, eight new kinds of camp with requests of their own, missions of the road, and peoples who remember single colonists.
- People's lives: personal stories of three, life goals that grow up with a child, more for the young and the old.
- More answers play out on the map: work parties, walks, vigils, searches, errands and hunts (only safe game, and nothing someone else has claimed).
- Wall lamps chosen by room size and hung on a real wall; mods round three (Outland, Medieval Total Rim, Alpha Genes and Memes); a lighter pass on the game.

## Setbacks, promises and This week

*16 October 2026, morning*

- The council window opens on This week: what waits on you, what's coming and how people are. Everyone is one sortable table of the colonists, and Dilemmas keeps every question and how each answer went.
- 41 setbacks: bad moods that come from what happens in play, each with a hint about what helps.
- Promises kept: a granted wish can be taken back, and colonists notice.
- Good moods are smaller and fewer; many small good moments now give something else (rest, recreation, a lesson, trust or a feeling).
- Rimfolk's systems react to each other, and colonists say aloud how they feel, talk about the weather and keep small habits (a grave visited, the stars watched, a slow walk).
- A balance pass from a simulated colony year, and your other mods in Rimfolk's newer systems.

## The calendar, personal stories and answers that play out

*15 October 2026*

- The colony calendar: days that matter, kept each year.
- Sixteen personal stories that unfold over seasons, and health stories: convalescence, sickbed visits, doctors' and midwives' records.
- Answers play out on the map: when a council answer says people feast, light a fire, raise a toast or visit a grave, they do.
- What the world's peoples remember of the colony, and events that follow from it.
- A settings window with pages, search and presets; one evening letter for the day's smaller news; room themes and advice when the colony outgrows a room.
- The breadth round: more secrets, vendettas, offices, customs, names, scenarios and feelings, and 1,830 more lines for the thinnest pools.
- A more readable Almanac, with tables and a text size.

## RimWorld 1.7, ailments, and more to say

*14 October 2026*

- Rimfolk runs on RimWorld 1.7 as well as 1.6, with one file for both.
- Everyday ailments (colds, strained backs, sunburn) and a rare tier of serious illnesses.
- Visual and sound touches from the game's own effects: sky tints, fireflies, camp smoke, celebrations.
- About 1,700 new lines, and a memory that keeps a line from coming round again too soon.
- Good times turn ordinary: good memories fade sooner, and standards rise to cover the game's comforts.
- Room plans: 28 room types and 108 layouts. A welcome letter, a "Start here" page and a self-check.
- More wishes, council projects, life goals, dilemmas, encounters, decrees and quirks, and a soft integration for PokéWorld.

## Meetings that happen, and rooms of every size

*13 October 2026*

- Council meetings are announced, colonists are called in, and those who miss one miss it for a reason.
- Rewards are earned instead of handed out, and everyday pressures push back on a colony that has it easy.
- Rooms: sizes and styles, eighteen new rooms, your own saved rooms, and how much of each material you have in the build menu.

## Everyday life and the heart

*12 October 2026*

- Everyday journal entries drawn from what is on a colonist's mind.
- Everyday frictions with each other and with the home itself: sticking doors, leaks, smoke and clutter.
- Feelings and strain: a layer under mood that builds through hard times and eases with rest, friends and quiet.
- Camps on the colony's map, region-wide situations, storyteller events, story chains and missions.
- Integrations with home-life and people mods: Dubs Bad Hygiene, Hospitality, Vanilla Cooking and Brewing, Outposts, RimHUD, A RimWorld of Magic and more.
- Every Almanac page rewritten in a shape of its own, and the text read through for a plainer voice.

## Strangers and the colony's character

*11 October 2026*

- Encounters with wildlife, visitors, traders, prisoners and fallen enemies; nemeses who come back, and pets with moments of their own.
- 27 new wishes, 72 in all, and 38 moments between colonists.
- The colony's character: what the council keeps choosing shapes who the colony is.
- A Mood tab for each colonist, a Moods page for the colony, and half and quarter speed for unhurried planning.
- World events, six scenarios, Almanac pages for many popular mods, and install or update with a double-click.

## From Colony Council to Rimfolk

*10 October 2026*

- The first versions: an advisor, wishes and the council's answers, meetings with speeches and votes, ambitions, the room planner and the Almanac.
- Decrees, dilemmas, traditions, offices and yearly elections.
- Journals, yearly annals, anniversaries and names earned by deeds.
- Friendships, rivalries and mentors, dreams and omens, life goals, secrets, vendettas, eulogies, quirks and heirlooms, all on a Story tab for each colonist.
