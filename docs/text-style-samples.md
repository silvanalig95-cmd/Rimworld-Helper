# Rimfolk's text: samples and plain rewrites (14 November 2026)

The player: "when you write text for the mod, especially when it's supposed to be flavour, i'd like you to be more neutral,
descriptive and "reasonable", over trying to make the text be creative, as this just most of the time sounds awkward. look
through a selection of text snippets from all kinds of elements from the mod, and then put next to it a rewritten version
using the above instructions."

The rule (now in CLAUDE.md): say what happens, who does it and why, in ordinary words. No poetic turns, riddling phrasing,
invented idioms, lofty or archaic voice, and no twist at the end of a line. Speech sounds like a person talking.

The samples were drawn at random from every part of the mod, with a few picked from the newest work. Placeholders such as
{A_nameDef} stay as they are. Nothing in the game has been changed yet; this is the comparison the player asked for.

## What makes the current text sound awkward

1. **Riddling or cut-short lines** that leave the reader to guess what is meant ("The floor is thin today.", "On days
   like this, it picks itself.").
2. **Things given feelings or will** ("the ground feels like it's listening", "it keeps count").
3. **A wry twist at the end** ("They know each other's snoring.", "When I try, it looks like an accident.").
4. **Archaic voice** in the medieval letters ("needeth", "meaneth", "Most dear ... whom we hold in great affection").
5. **In-world phrases that are never explained** ("wrote the colony in good ink, with the mark of a friend's keeping").
6. **Long lists strung together with colons and semicolons**, where two or three plain sentences would read better.
7. **Atmosphere in place of information** ("Nobody can say why.", "the birds follow the dust").

## Already plain: what the rewrites aim for

These were in the sample and already read the way the player asks. They need no change, or one word.

- "My wrists are swollen. I dropped a cup this morning." (a mood)
- "Over a bowl of soup, {X_nameDef} told {A_nameDef} that two warlords up north are burning each other's fields again.
  Grain will be dear by winter." ("dear" could become "expensive".)
- "The bounty hunters have come for {A_nameDef}. They won't leave without a fight." (an event)
- "The people at the camp did us a good turn and asked for nothing in return." (a mood)
- "Everyone works longer, harder days until the decree ends. Building, harvesting and crafting go faster, at a cost in
  mood." (a decree)
- "Somebody else will have to finish this, I've had enough for today." (a bubble)

## A full pass, if the player wants it

The mod holds many thousands of lines. Rewriting all of them in this style would go area by area, each its own release.
Each round would go through the text checks, and each changed line would keep its tags and placeholders. The order:

1. The gods (presences, happenings, the book)
2. Letters, including dropping the archaic medieval voice for plain, slightly formal English
3. Moods, memories and dreams
4. Encounters and camps
5. The region, events and decrees
6. The peoples' talk and events (the faction descriptions are already a to-do of their own)
7. Bubbles and wishes
8. The Almanac and settings

From now on, all new text is written this way.
