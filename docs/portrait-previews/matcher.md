# The portrait library's matcher: who gets which picture

sheets: the first wave's prompt list (`docs/portrait-art/prompts.csv`) is read as a library, each row a face of its own
with the painted mid-tones of its words (skin s1 #f1d6c2 to s5 #5a3a28), and each sample person, with their skin and
hair in the game's own colours, is matched against the trial's 16 pictures and against all 200. The score is the
distance (0 is their exact look); with "the closest" (the setting's first choice) the nearest is shown however far, with "only close
ones" a picture further than 6 gives way to the game's own face. The last column gives the nearest three of the 200.

The two men who look alike get different faces: of the nearest few, each person gets the nearest of those fewest others keep, then
one by their own id.
A kind with no pictures of its own keeps the game's own face, however human it looks (the hussar, the impid, the dark
elf), and is written in the library folder's missing list; once a kind's pictures are in, its people get only theirs. A person only
ever gets a picture of their own age band, with a skin of the same or the next tone, else none (the first wave has no
toddlers, so the girl of two keeps the game's own face); of those, the hair's colour counts most. A person keeps the
picture first chosen for good, whatever happens to their body or clothes; a child gets a new one when they grow into the
next stage.
