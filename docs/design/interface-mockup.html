Rimfolk · the interface for the long run · 4 November 2026

One map, few tabs, and every name a way in

You noticed the region window grew to ten tabs. Stage 2 would make it twelve. This is the plan to stop that for good: four windows as now, at most five views in each, a card for every person, town and people you can open from wherever its name appears, and one place for everything that waits on you. Below, the region window, a card reached from a name, This week as the inbox, the council window in the same language, the look, and the order to build it.

The hub

The region window: a map with lenses, a town's whole story beside it

Trading posts, raiders' camps and hamlets stop being pages. They become what the Trade, Threats and Ours lenses show on the same map, each with a list. Picking a town opens its card in the drawer on the right, which is where everything about that town lives.

Where a new thing goes

The rule for every feature still to come. Ask in this order and stop at the first yes.

  1. Does it wait on you? An answer, an offer, a call: a row in This week, with its act on the card of what it is about.
  2. Is it a thing? A person, place, people, house, tie, event or letter: it uses that card.
  3. Is it about a place? A section in the place's card, and marks in the lens that reads it.
  4. Is it a new way to look at what is already listed? A filter, or a mode of a view (five at most).
  5. Is it something the colony does? An act on a card, with why not, and in Talk with... if done face to face.
  6. Is it a record of what happened? A kind of history line: it shows in the timeline and in every card it names.
  7. Is it an explanation? An Almanac article, behind the view's "?".
  8. None of these, and opened every week? A view, if its window has fewer than five. A new window never.

The order

Five releases, before stage 2 begins

Each one plays as before. Every old page name still opens the right place, so keys, hints and buttons keep working.

  1. UI 1

    Names that lead somewhere

    Cards for every kind of thing, the drawer in the region window, links in the region's lists and its history, previews, back and forward. "Show" opens the town on the region map.

  2. UI 2

    The region in four views

    Map with six lenses and a list for each; Peoples with Faces; Here and away; History with the world's events at the top. Posts, camps and hamlets become lenses.

  3. UI 3

    One inbox, and search

    This week gathers everything waiting, shows what is under way in steps, and follows anything you pin. One count in every window's corner and on the council button. A search box finds anyone by name.

  4. UI 4

    The council in four views

    This week, People, Council and Colony, with modes; the person's card in the drawer; the Almanac in its own window with "?" on every view.

  5. UI 5

    One look

    The colours, sizes and parts across every window, Defense and Planning included; names in the inspect tabs as links; a second level of previews.

Your choices

Seven decisions

Decided on 4 November 2026: all seven as recommended.

  1. The region in four views, with trading posts, raiders' camps and hamlets as lenses, and stage 2's Diplomacy and Household as Peoples and Here and away.

    Recommended: yes, rather than grouping the old tabs.

  2. Where a card opens: in a drawer at the right of the window you are in, the region map's panel being that drawer.

    Recommended: the drawer, rather than a full page in another window.

  3. Previews after a short pause, kept when the mouse enters them, two levels at most, with a setting.

    Recommended: previews, rather than the game's plain tooltips only.

  4. The Almanac in its own movable window, reached by "?" on every view.

    Recommended: its own window, rather than the council's fourth section.

  5. The council window in four views with modes, dreams and journals in the person's card, priorities in Planning.

    Recommended: yes, rather than two rows of tabs.

  6. Window size: a setting to let the four windows grow on large screens, up to 1280 by 860.

    Recommended: the setting, standard size by default.

  7. Your colonies in stage 3: in the Ours lens with a card each and a colony chip in every view's header.

    Recommended: the Ours lens, rather than a page in the council's Colony section.
