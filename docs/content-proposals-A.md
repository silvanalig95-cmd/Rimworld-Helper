# Content proposals A: events, world situations, visitors, camps, strangers

A list to pick from, as the rules ask: nothing here is built. Another list covers illnesses, dilemmas, customs, wishes
and moods.

**What is there now.** 219 events (84 visitors, 40 challenges, 37 of the land, 31 from the past, 12 fights, 14
illnesses), 57 world situations, 23 kinds of camp, 214 stranger moments and 22 home nuisances. They lean low-tech and
social. Almost nothing yet touches Ideology's relics, Royalty's anima tree, Biotech's mechs and xenotypes, Anomaly, or
Odyssey's gravship and fishing. Few visitors or camps belong to an industrial or spacer world, though your colony has
electric benches, mechtech and gravtech. The colony's art, books, game tables, its animals' own lives and the enemy
dead after a raid are barely used.

**How each item is meant.** It only comes when the colony really has what it needs, the words describe what the map
shows, nobody is hurt for flavour, and other mods are read softly. "Map" names the existing play-out that shows it;
**NEW** marks a small play-out still to write. Effort: S (data and a small worker), M (a worker plus a small new
play-out), L (a new hook into a DLC or mod).

**If you only pick five:** 1 (art collector), 3 (undertakers), 13 (fishers), 15 (salvagers), 24 (orphaned young).

## Visitors

**1. The art collector** (M). A collector comes to see the colony's art, stops before each piece, and offers for one by
its title.
*Map:* walks the rooms from piece to piece (**NEW** visitor work "view", beside prospect, glean, sing and hunt); a sold
piece is carried out to them (Give); a commissioned piece is made at the sculptor's bench (PlayMake) and collected next
visit (a new Regular kind, "collector"). *When:* three works of Good quality or better. *Choices:* sell (silver well
above value; the maker is proud or hurt by temperament), keep it (the colony's name travels), commission a new work
(a sculptor's day, paid by quality). *Needs:* core.

**2. A wedding at the colony** (M). A neighbouring couple asks to marry at the colony: its marriage spot or its biggest
hall, a feast, a night under its roofs.
*Map:* the families walk in (VisitorTrip), the gathering is held with them as guests (PlayGuests) with the couple at
its head (PlayListen's head place), a feast cooked at the colony's stove (PlayCook), the night in guest beds
(VisitorBeds) or round a fire they lay (VisitorCamp). *When:* a people with goodwill 20 or more; a dining room or a
marriage spot. *Choices:* host and feed them (meals spent; silver, goodwill, a joy for those who come), host if they
bring the food (their people carry it in), decline. *Needs:* core; Ideology and Hospitality used when present.

**3. The undertakers** (M). After a raid, a burial order of a friendly people offers to take the enemy dead off the
colony's hands.
*Map:* they carry the bodies away down the road (Borne, as road mourners do), or dig graves outside the wall (PlayDead,
**NEW**: dug by visitors). *When:* four or more enemy dead still lying outside a day after a fight. *Choices:* graves
for silver; for free if they keep the raiders' gear (a real loss of loot against hours of hauling); refuse.
*Needs:* core.

**4. Kin come for a captive** (M). Family of one of the colony's prisoners (or, with Ideology, slaves) comes to the gate
with silver for their freedom.
*Map:* they wait at the gate (VisitEffect_AtGate); a released prisoner is walked out with the game's own release job
and leaves with them (PlayDepart); "let them talk first" brings one of them to the cell door (**NEW**, small).
*When:* a prisoner held five days or more; a slave of some months. *Choices:* take the silver, free them for nothing
(goodwill, mercy), let them talk first (resistance or will falls), refuse (it rises). *Needs:* core; Ideology for slaves.

**5. The traveling barber** (S/M). A barber sets a stool by the gate and offers each colonist a new cut.
*Map:* a new VisitorServices kind beside the tinker's mending and the doctor's doses: the barber goes round, sits each
one a while, and their hair or beard really changes to a style their ideoligion or their own taste likes; at Vanilla
Hair Expanded's barber table if one is built. *When:* any friendly people, three or more colonists. *Choices:*
everyone (silver a head), only those who want it (some refuse by trait), decline. A good cut gives a small social
reward rather than a mood. *Needs:* core; Ideology styles and the hair mods widen the choice.

**6. The animal doctor** (M). A drover's doctor offers to see to the colony's sick and hurt animals, and to work one
young animal for a morning.
*Map:* goes pen to pen and tends each (**NEW**: the outsider-tend job turned round, the visitor tending), then a training
session with the game's training effect, one step given. *When:* a colony animal hurt, ill, or with a trainable it
hasn't learned. *Choices:* pay silver, pay in feed carried out, trade it for a bed, decline. Comes back as a Regular
("vet"). *Needs:* core; Misc. Training's trainables count.

**7. Pilgrims to the relic** (M). People of the colony's own faith walk a long way to kneel before its relic.
*Map:* a procession of the visitors to the reliquary and back (Procession, with guests), kneeling there with the camps'
prayer job. *When:* Ideology; a relic in a reliquary; a friendly people sharing the ideoligion. *Choices:* open the
doors (goodwill, certainty for the devout, strangers inside the walls), pray at the gate only, ask an offering
(silver; the devout mind), turn them away. *Needs:* Ideology.

**8. Pilgrims at the anima tree** (M). Psycasters and tribal folk ask to meditate at the colony's anima tree.
*Map:* they sit in a ring facing the tree, and plant anima grass sprigs there (real plants that raise the tree's
meditation bonus); a colony psycaster may sit a session with them (Visit). *When:* Royalty; an anima tree on the map.
*Choices:* welcome them, charge a fee (they plant less), keep them off. *Needs:* Royalty.

**9. Kin of the blood** (M). People of a colonist's own xenotype hear one of theirs lives here and come to meet them.
*Map:* they ask at the gate by name (AtGate), an evening at the fire with them as guests (Bonfire with guests), a lesson
in the skill their genes favour (Lesson). *When:* Biotech; a non-baseliner colonist; a friendly people whose xenotypes
include theirs (Stoneborn, Boglegs, Avaloi, Korg, elves, merren and the rest from your mods). *Choices:* host them,
let the colonist go with them for a few days (a Mission: labour lost, they come back with something), a word at the
gate. *Needs:* Biotech.

**10. Gawkers at the gravship** (M/L). Where the colony's gravship comes down near a settlement, locals walk up to see
it, children in tow.
*Map:* a walk round the hull with them (Procession, **NEW**: a route round a building), local goods offered at the gate
(Spawn by Outsiders). *When:* Odyssey; a gravship landing (the game's PostGravshipLanded) within a few tiles of a
friendly settlement. *Choices:* a tour (goodwill, the colony's name travels), trade only, keep them back (they watch
from the edge and go). *Needs:* Odyssey.

**11. The book-swapper** (S/M). A reader with a pack of books offers to swap one of theirs for one of the colony's.
*Map:* the colony's book is carried out (Give), theirs set down at the gate (Spawn by Outsiders); or they sit a day
copying one at the colony's bookcase (**NEW** visitor work "read"). *When:* books in the colony. *Choices:* swap, lend
it for copying (goodwill), sell it, decline. *Needs:* core books; Vanilla Books Expanded's count.

**12. Regulars' own lives** (S/M). The visitors the colony knows come back changed: married, with a child, old and
handing their trade to an apprentice, or word comes that they died on the road.
*Map:* the regular walks in with the new person (VisitorTrip "with"); a keepsake left for their friend is carried in
(Give). *When:* a Regular with three visits and a warm bond. *Choices:* a gift for the wedding or the child, take on
the apprentice as the new Regular, mourn them (their friend's vigil). *Needs:* core.

## Camps

**13. Fishers** (M). A fishing family camps on the bank for a few days, fishing from dawn and drying the catch at
their fire.
*Map:* **NEW** camp work "fish" (at the water with the game's fishing effect; the catch goes to their stash and their
fire). The water's fish stock really falls while they fish. *When:* river, lake or coast with fish; Fishing
researched. *Choices:* buy their fish (CampMarket), ask where they bite (they show the richest water: Show, suggestion
only), ask them to fish the far water or move on (tension). *Needs:* Odyssey, or Vanilla Fishing Expanded alone.

**14. Salvagers** (M). A scrap crew camps by the ancient ruins on the colony's land and takes them apart.
*Map:* **NEW** camp work "strip": the game's deconstruct effect at each piece, then the piece is gone and steel and slag
pile up at their camp; a share carried to the colony's edge (CampCarry). *When:* industrial colony or later; ancient
walls, junk or mech remains outside the home area. *Choices:* split the haul, pay them to clear the ruin nearest the
colony, "these are ours" (they leave, or pay a fee to finish). *Needs:* core; Dismantle Ancient Junk and Real Ruins add
pieces.

**15. Harvest hands** (M). A reaping crew camps by the fields at the end of summer and offers to bring the harvest in.
*Map:* **NEW** work "reap": they cut ripe plants with the game's harvest effect and leave the crop for the colony's
haulers; their share is carried off when they go. *When:* ripe crops more than the colony's growers can bring in before
the first frost (from the fields, the growers' skill and the WeatherBook). *Choices:* one sheaf in ten, silver, a hot
meal each night (CarryOut, eaten at the camp), decline. *Needs:* core.

**16. Spacers down for repairs** (M). A small crew whose landing craft came down at the map's edge camps by the hull.
*Map:* the hull as a camp structure (a Royalty shuttle if present); a colony crafter works at it (Visit with the working
effect); at the end the shuttle lifts off, or they walk off and leave the hull as salvage. *When:* the colony is
industrial or later; a spacer people in the world. *Choices:* sell components, plasteel and steel from the stores
(paid in advanced components or glitterworld medicine), lend a mechanic for a day, take in the passenger who asks to
stay. *Needs:* core; Royalty for the shuttle.

**17. Watchers of the monolith** (M). Quiet scholars camp within sight of the woken monolith and keep watch at night.
*Map:* a night vigil at the monolith (the camps' vigil job). *When:* Anomaly; the monolith woken. *Choices:* share notes
(the game's own study knowledge for the colony), shelter them in a storm (guest beds), ask them to leave. The colony
weighs knowledge against strangers near something dangerous. *Needs:* Anomaly.

**18. The beast show** (M). Keepers travel with tame beasts of the world in a pen at their camp.
*Map:* a pen at the camp (camp structures), the beasts led round by day (the camps' herd job), the children's visit to
the camp (Visit). *When:* any; the animal mods widen the kinds. *Choices:* buy one of their tame beasts
(SpawnAnimal), sell them one of the colony's (its keeper minds), let the children help feed them (a lesson in
Animals). *Needs:* core.

## World situations

**19. The run** (M). In spring or autumn the fish come up the river or into the bay in numbers.
*Map:* the water's fish stock is raised for the season (the game's own), the fishers' camp (13) is drawn, a fish
supper at the fire (Feast). Beats: the first big catch, a quarrel over the best water, the run thinning out.
*When:* water with fish; Fishing researched. *Needs:* Odyssey or Vanilla Fishing Expanded.

**20. Market days** (S/M). For a fortnight the neighbours make the colony's market their own.
*Map:* buyers come in waves to the colony's stalls (VisitorTrade's shopping, as campers do now), a traders' camp asks
for a pitch by the gate, a bulk order (the Order event), a quarrel over a price (the Dispute event). *When:* RimMarket
stalls stocked; friendly neighbours within reach. *Choices:* a stall for the traders or not, the order taken or not,
whose side in the quarrel. *Needs:* RimMarket.

**21. Busy orbital lanes** (S/M). A season of heavy traffic overhead: orbital traders call more often and one sends
an agent down in person.
*Map:* the agent lands (a shuttle with Royalty, else a drop pod) to buy one named thing from the stores, carried out
to them (CarryOut); falling debris and a strange signal are drawn (the existing Starfall and Signal events).
*When:* a comms console built. *Choices:* sell the named thing, haggle, refuse. *Needs:* core.

**22. The assize** (M). A circuit judge holds court in the nearest town for a few weeks.
*Map:* the judge's clerk at the gate (AtGate); a prisoner handed over walks out with them (the game's release job);
the judge stays a night (Stay, guest bed). Verdicts are told in words; nothing grim is shown. *When:* a friendly town
nearby. *Choices:* hand over a prisoner (silver, goodwill), bring a grievance (the neighbours' quarrel decided either
way), keep away. *Needs:* core; prisoners make it richer.

## Colony events

**23. Orphaned young** (S/M). After a hunt, a young animal of the same kind stays by the kill.
*Map:* the young one stands by the body (spawned if none was near; the words say it was there); tamed with the game's
own taming (Designate), or hand-fed at the pens (HandOver's Feed). *When:* a colonist killed a wild animal whose kind
has young; not a big predator. *Choices:* tame it, raise it by hand, leave it (the hunter's thought by temperament).
*Needs:* core.

**24. A wild one at the pens** (S). A wild animal of the colony's own livestock kind (a boar by the pigs, a wild horse
by the horses) hangs about the pens at dusk.
*Map:* starts a ResidentBeast story for it (seen, named, watched); tame (Designate) or drive off (DriveOff).
*When:* livestock whose kind lives wild in this biome. *Choices:* tame it into the herd, let it be, drive it off.
*Needs:* core; the animal mods widen the kinds.

**25. Lost in the storm** (M). A thunderclap sends one of the colony's grazing animals bolting for the far side of the
map.
*Map:* the animal's flight (**NEW**: a flee toward the edge); a search when it's safe (Search), then walked home on a
rope (the Herd work). Left out too long, it goes wild (the game's own). *When:* a thunderstorm or blizzard; roaming or
penned animals. *Choices:* search now in the weather, at first light, or let it go. *Needs:* core.

**26. A rich seam** (S/M). A miner breaks into a pocket of ore deep in the rock.
*Map:* the ore cells are really there beside the cell being mined (**NEW**: set in the rock); "follow it" marks them for
mining. *When:* a colonist mining under overhead mountain. *Choices:* follow the seam (more of the mountain opened
up, and with it the game's real risk of insects under deep rock), mine only the pocket, wall it off for later.
*Needs:* core.

**27. A game for a stake** (S/M). A visitor challenges the colony's best at its chess or poker table.
*Map:* the two sit across the game table (PlaySit) for an hour while the colony gathers to watch (Gathering in the
rec room); the outcome by skill and luck (Contest). *When:* a game table built; a visitor or Regular on the map.
*Choices:* play for silver, for a thing of theirs against one of the colony's, for the fun of it (recreation for the
watchers), decline. *Needs:* core.

**28. A commission** (M). A neighbour wants one fine made thing the colony can make: a coat, a rifle, a carved chair.
*Map:* the bill is set at the right bench with a minimum quality (the Making page's own Set bill); when it's done the
colony sends word and their rider collects it (CarryOut). *When:* the bench built and the research done. *Choices:*
take it (a crafter's hours for silver and the colony's name; a masterwork brings their custom back), take it at a
lower quality for less, decline. *Needs:* core.

## Stranger moments

**29. The colony's mechs** (S/M). A new stranger kind: small moments between colonists and the colony's own mechs (a
child names the lifter, someone talks to the agrihand down the rows, a mechanitor's quiet hour with a damaged mech).
Rewards rather than moods. *When:* Biotech; colony mechs near the colonist. *Needs:* Biotech.

**30. Slaves** (S/M). A new stranger kind: a kind word at work, a song from home, a hard word from the overseer, with
effects on suppression, will and opinion that follow the colony's own precepts on slavery. *When:* Ideology; a slave
near the colonist. *Needs:* Ideology.

**31. At the holding platforms** (S). A new stranger kind for contained entities: a researcher's fascination
(Intellectual practice), a colonist's unease, a child's question. *When:* Anomaly; an entity held. *Needs:* Anomaly.

---

**Left out on purpose:** anything built on weapon duels, Diplomatic Marriage or Factional War (declined before);
pollution upstream (Biotech's world pollution can't be read reliably from a neighbour); a beached whale (it would
hurt an animal for flavour); a mechanitor's camp whose mechs follow a visitor (fragile in the game's AI).
