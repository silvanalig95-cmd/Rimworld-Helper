# Content proposals A: events, world situations, visitors, camps, strangers

A list to pick from, as the rules ask: nothing here is built. Another list covers illnesses, dilemmas, customs, wishes
and moods.

**What is there now.** 204 events (84 visitors, 40 challenges, 37 of the land, 31 from the past, 12 fights) and 13
illnesses, 57 world situations, 23 kinds of camp, 214 stranger moments, 22 home nuisances. They lean low-tech and
social. Almost nothing touches Ideology's relics, Royalty's anima tree, Biotech's mechs and xenotypes, Anomaly, or
Odyssey's gravship and fishing; few visitors or camps fit an industrial or spacer colony like yours. The colony's
art, books, game tables, its animals' lives and the enemy dead after a raid are barely used.

**How to read an item.** It only comes when the colony has what it needs; the words match the map; nobody is hurt
for flavour; mods are read softly. "Map" names the existing play-out that shows it; **NEW** marks a small play-out
still to write. Effort: S (data, small worker), M (worker plus a small play-out), L (a new DLC or mod hook).

**If you only pick five:** 1 (art collector), 3 (undertakers), 13 (fishers), 14 (salvagers), 23 (orphaned young).

## Visitors

**1. The art collector** (M). A collector comes to see the colony's art and offers for one piece by its title.
*Map:* walks the rooms, stopping before each piece (**NEW** visitor work "view", beside prospect, glean, sing, hunt);
a sold piece is carried out (Give); a commission is made at the sculptor's bench (PlayMake) and collected next visit
(new Regular kind "collector"). *When:* three works of Good quality or better. *Choices:* sell (well above value; the
maker proud or hurt by temperament), keep it (the colony's name travels), commission one (a sculptor's day, paid by
quality). *Needs:* core.

**2. A wedding at the colony** (M). A neighbouring couple asks to marry at the colony, with a feast and a night there.
*Map:* a gathering with the families as guests (PlayGuests), the couple at its head (PlayListen); a feast cooked at
the colony's stove (PlayCook); the night in guest beds (VisitorBeds) or round their own fire (VisitorCamp). *When:*
goodwill 20 or more; a dining room or marriage spot. *Choices:* host and feed them (meals for silver, goodwill, joy),
host if they bring the food, decline. *Needs:* core; uses Ideology and Hospitality when present.

**3. The undertakers** (M). After a raid, a burial order of a friendly people offers to take the enemy dead away.
*Map:* they carry the bodies off down the road (Borne, as road mourners do) or dig graves outside the wall (PlayDead,
**NEW**: dug by visitors). *When:* four or more enemy dead still lying out a day after a fight. *Choices:* graves for
silver; free if they keep the raiders' gear (loot lost against hours of hauling); refuse. *Needs:* core.

**4. Kin come for a captive** (M). Family of a prisoner (or, with Ideology, a slave) brings silver for their freedom.
*Map:* they wait at the gate (AtGate); a freed prisoner is walked out with the game's own release job and leaves with
them (PlayDepart); one of them may be shown to the cell door (**NEW**, small). *When:* a prisoner held five days, a
slave of some months. *Choices:* take the silver, free them for nothing (goodwill), let them talk first (resistance
or will falls), refuse (it rises). *Needs:* core; Ideology for slaves.

**5. The traveling barber** (S/M). A barber sets a stool by the gate and offers each colonist a new cut.
*Map:* a new VisitorServices kind beside the tinker's mending: the barber sits each one a while, and their hair or
beard really changes to a style their ideoligion or taste likes (at Vanilla Hair Expanded's barber table if built).
*When:* any friendly people. *Choices:* everyone (silver a head), only those who want it (some refuse by trait),
decline; a small social reward, not a mood. *Needs:* core; Ideology and the hair mods widen the styles.

**6. The animal doctor** (M). A drovers' doctor offers to see to the colony's hurt and sick animals and to work one
young animal for a morning.
*Map:* goes pen to pen tending each (**NEW**: the outsider-tend job turned round), then a training session with the
game's training effect, one step given. *When:* an animal hurt, ill, or short of a trainable. *Choices:* silver, feed
carried out, a bed for the night, decline. Returns as a Regular ("vet"). *Needs:* core; Misc. Training counts.

**7. Pilgrims to the relic** (M). People of the colony's faith walk a long way to kneel before its relic.
*Map:* a procession of the visitors to the reliquary and back (Procession with guests), kneeling with the camps'
prayer job. *When:* a relic in a reliquary; a friendly people of the same ideoligion. *Choices:* open the doors
(goodwill, certainty, strangers inside the walls), the gate only, ask an offering (the devout mind), turn them away.
*Needs:* Ideology.

**8. Pilgrims at the anima tree** (M). Psycasters and tribal folk ask to meditate at the colony's anima tree.
*Map:* they sit in a ring facing it and plant anima grass there (real plants, which the tree's linking ritual needs);
a colony psycaster may sit with them (Visit). *When:* an anima tree on the map. *Choices:* welcome them, charge a fee
(they plant less), keep them off. *Needs:* Royalty.

**9. Kin of the blood** (M). People of a colonist's own xenotype hear one of theirs lives here and come to meet them.
*Map:* they ask at the gate by name, an evening at the fire as guests (Bonfire), a lesson in the skill their genes
favour (Lesson). *When:* a non-baseliner colonist; a friendly people whose xenotypes include theirs (Stoneborn,
Boglegs, Avaloi, Korg, elves, merren and the rest). *Choices:* host them, let the colonist go with them a few days
(a Mission: labour lost, back with something), a word at the gate. *Needs:* Biotech.

**10. Gawkers at the gravship** (M/L). Where the gravship comes down near a settlement, locals walk up to see it.
*Map:* a walk round the hull with them (Procession, **NEW**: a route round a building), local goods offered at the
gate. *When:* a landing (the game's PostGravshipLanded) a few tiles from a friendly settlement. *Choices:* a tour
(goodwill, renown), trade only, keep them back. *Needs:* Odyssey.

**11. The book-swapper** (S/M). A reader with a pack of books offers one of theirs for one of the colony's.
*Map:* the colony's book carried out (Give), theirs set down at the gate; or a day copying one at the colony's
bookcase (**NEW** visitor work "read"). *When:* books in the colony. *Choices:* swap, lend it for copying (goodwill),
sell it, decline. *Needs:* core; Vanilla Books Expanded's books count.

**12. Regulars' own lives** (S/M). Known visitors come back changed: married, with a child, old and handing their
trade to an apprentice; or word comes they died on the road.
*Map:* the regular walks in with the new person; a keepsake for their friend is carried in (Give). *When:* a Regular
with three visits and a warm bond. *Choices:* a gift for the wedding or the child, take the apprentice on as the new
Regular, a vigil for the dead. *Needs:* core.

## Camps

**13. Fishers** (M). A fishing family camps on the bank, fishing from dawn and drying the catch at their fire.
*Map:* **NEW** camp work "fish" (at the water with the game's fishing effect; the catch to their stash and fire). The
water's fish stock really falls while they fish. *When:* river, lake or coast with fish; Fishing researched.
*Choices:* buy their fish (CampMarket), ask where they bite (they show the richest water: a suggestion with Show),
ask them to fish the far water or move on (tension). *Needs:* Odyssey, or Vanilla Fishing Expanded alone.

**14. Salvagers** (M). A scrap crew camps by the ancient ruins on the colony's land and takes them apart.
*Map:* **NEW** camp work "strip": the game's deconstruct effect at each piece, then it is gone and steel and slag pile
up at their camp; a share carried to the colony's edge (CampCarry). *When:* industrial colony or later; ancient walls,
junk or mech remains outside the home area. *Choices:* split the haul, pay them to clear the ruin nearest the colony,
"these are ours" (they leave, or pay to finish). *Needs:* core; Dismantle Ancient Junk and Real Ruins add pieces.

**15. Harvest hands** (M). A reaping crew camps by the fields at summer's end and offers to bring the harvest in.
*Map:* **NEW** work "reap": they cut ripe plants with the game's harvest effect and leave the crop for the colony's
haulers; their share goes with them. *When:* more ripe crop than the growers can bring in before the first frost
(fields, growers' skill, the WeatherBook). *Choices:* one sheaf in ten, silver, a hot meal each night (CarryOut),
decline. *Needs:* core.

**16. Spacers down for repairs** (M). A crew whose landing craft came down at the map's edge camps by the hull.
*Map:* the hull as a camp structure (a Royalty shuttle if present); a colony crafter works at it (Visit); at the end
the shuttle lifts off, or they walk off leaving the hull as salvage. *When:* industrial colony or later; a spacer
people in the world. *Choices:* sell components, plasteel, steel (for advanced components or glitterworld medicine),
lend a mechanic for a day, take in a passenger who asks to stay. *Needs:* core; Royalty for the shuttle.

**17. Watchers of the monolith** (M). Quiet scholars camp within sight of the woken monolith and keep watch at night.
*Map:* a night vigil at the monolith (the camps' vigil job). *When:* the monolith woken. *Choices:* share notes (the
game's own study knowledge), shelter them in a storm (guest beds), ask them to leave: knowledge against strangers
near something dangerous. *Needs:* Anomaly.

**18. The beast show** (M). Keepers travel with tame beasts of the world in a pen at their camp.
*Map:* a pen at the camp, the beasts led round by day (the camps' herd job), the children's visit (Visit). *When:*
any; the animal mods widen the kinds. *Choices:* buy one of their beasts (SpawnAnimal), sell one of the colony's (its
keeper minds), let the children help feed them (practice in Animals). *Needs:* core.

## World situations

**19. The run** (M). In spring or autumn the fish come up the river or into the bay in numbers.
*Map:* the water's fish stock raised for the season (the game's own), the fishers' camp (13) drawn, a fish supper at
the fire (Feast). Beats: the first big catch, a quarrel over the best water, the run thinning. *When:* water with
fish; Fishing researched. *Needs:* Odyssey or Vanilla Fishing Expanded.

**20. Market days** (S/M). For a fortnight the neighbours make the colony's market their own.
*Map:* buyers come in waves to the colony's stalls (VisitorTrade's shopping), a traders' camp asks for a pitch, a bulk
order (the Order event), a quarrel over a price (the Dispute event). *When:* stocked RimMarket stalls. *Choices:* the
pitch, the order, whose side in the quarrel. *Needs:* RimMarket.

**21. Busy orbital lanes** (S/M). Orbital traders call more often, and one sends an agent down in person.
*Map:* the agent lands (a shuttle with Royalty, else a drop pod) to buy one named thing, carried out to them
(CarryOut); falling debris and a strange signal are drawn (the Starfall and Signal events). *When:* a comms console.
*Choices:* sell, haggle, refuse. *Needs:* core.

**22. The assize** (M). A circuit judge holds court in the nearest town for a few weeks.
*Map:* the clerk at the gate (AtGate); a prisoner handed over walks out with them (the game's release job); the judge
stays a night (Stay). Verdicts are told in words; nothing grim is shown. *When:* a friendly town nearby. *Choices:*
hand over a prisoner (silver, goodwill), bring a grievance (the neighbours' quarrel decided), keep away. *Needs:* core.

## Colony events

**23. Orphaned young** (S/M). After a hunt, a young animal of the same kind stays by the kill.
*Map:* the young one stands by the body (spawned if none was near: the words say it was there); tamed with the game's
taming (Designate) or hand-fed at the pens (HandOver's Feed). *When:* a colonist killed a wild animal whose kind has
young; not a big predator. *Choices:* tame it, raise it by hand, leave it (the hunter's thought by temperament).
*Needs:* core.

**24. A wild one at the pens** (S). A wild animal of the colony's own livestock kind hangs about the pens at dusk.
*Map:* starts a ResidentBeast story for it (seen, named, watched); tame (Designate) or drive off (DriveOff). *When:*
livestock whose kind lives wild here. *Choices:* tame it, let it be, drive it off. *Needs:* core; animal mods widen it.

**25. A rich seam** (S/M). A miner breaks into a pocket of ore deep in the rock.
*Map:* the ore is really there beside the cell being mined (**NEW**: set in the rock); "follow it" marks it for mining.
*When:* a colonist mining under overhead mountain. *Choices:* follow the seam (more mountain opened, and with it the
game's real risk of insects under deep rock), take only the pocket, wall it off for later. *Needs:* core.

**26. A game for a stake** (S/M). A visitor challenges the colony's best at its chess or poker table.
*Map:* the two sit across the table (PlaySit) for an hour while the colony gathers to watch (Gathering in the rec
room); skill and luck decide (Contest). *When:* a game table; a visitor or Regular on the map. *Choices:* play for
silver, for a thing against a thing, for fun (recreation for the watchers), decline. *Needs:* core.

**27. A commission** (M). A neighbour wants one fine made thing the colony can make: a coat, a rifle, a carved chair.
*Map:* the bill is set at the right bench with a minimum quality (the Making page's Set bill); when it's done the
colony sends word and their rider collects it (CarryOut). *When:* bench built, research done. *Choices:* take it (a
crafter's hours for silver; a masterwork brings their custom back), take it at a lower quality for less, decline.
*Needs:* core.

## Stranger moments

**28. The colony's mechs** (S/M). A new stranger kind: a child names the lifter, someone talks to the agrihand down
the rows, a mechanitor's quiet hour with a damaged mech. Rewards rather than moods. *When:* colony mechs nearby.
*Needs:* Biotech.

**29. Slaves** (S/M). A new stranger kind: a kind word at work, a song from home, a hard word from the overseer, with
effects on suppression, will and opinion that follow the colony's own precepts. *When:* a slave nearby. *Needs:*
Ideology.

**30. At the holding platforms** (S). A new stranger kind for contained entities: a researcher's fascination
(Intellectual practice), a colonist's unease, a child's question. *When:* an entity held. *Needs:* Anomaly.

---

**Left out on purpose:** weapon duels, Diplomatic Marriage, Factional War (declined before); pollution upstream
(can't be read reliably); a beached whale (hurts an animal for flavour); an animal bolting in a storm (the handlers
rope it home anyway: no real choice); a mechanitor's camp (visitors' mechs are fragile in the game's AI).
