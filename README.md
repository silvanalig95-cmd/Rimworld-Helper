# RimWorld Helper

A RimWorld mod idea: the colony tells you what it needs, your colonists tell you what
they want, and you decide.

- **Advisor**: ranks what your colony needs next (food before winter, defenses vs. wealth, bedrooms, power, etc.) and explains why.
- **Pawn wishes**: colonists make occasional requests based on their traits, passions, relationships and mood ("I want my own room", "I want a proper workshop"). Fulfil them for a mood boost.
- **Colony meetings**: every season, pick one bigger ambition to work toward.
