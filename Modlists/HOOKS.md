# Integration ideas from the mod research

The most promising ways Rimfolk could work with the mods in the main medieval modlist, gathered from the notes in
`mods/`. Each note has the details (def names, classes, events) and more ideas. Done items are marked.

## Fixed already (found during the research)
- Era detection: Medieval Overhaul's watermill no longer counts as electricity.
- Wishes: Medieval Overhaul's raw wood (firewood), rings (jewelry), hearth (a fire of one's own); research shelves no
  longer count as bookcases; Vanilla Furniture Expanded's silver-bought props no longer count as the real thing;
  Dubs Bad Hygiene's burn pit and sauna stove aren't a bedroom fire; soy sauce isn't brewing, the vodka drum is.
- Vanilla Skills Expanded and Alpha Skills passions are read from their definitions.
- Vehicle Framework passengers in flight are still the colony's.
- Long-lived genes (Stoneborn, elves) age at their own pace.
- Hospitality guests can't be thrown out by the council.
- The advisor explains Vanilla Events Expanded, Alpha Biomes and Seasonal Weather world events.
- Taming self-help and the predator den dilemma stay away from dangerous beasts.
- Danger is read from combat power too: the hunting wish picks only ordinary game, a beast nemesis too big to take
  on alone waits for the council's order, and a creature size limit also caps how hard it fights.
- Xenotypes that never sleep or never eat aren't asked about beds or meals; long-lived genes stretch only the adult
  years, so a grown dwarf isn't read as a teenager.

## Medieval life (Medieval Overhaul, Rimedieval, Medieval Total Rim, Divine Order)
- Wine and cheese aging as council material: a vintners' ambition, a dilemma about opening the legendary cask for a
  wedding, a "proper cellar" wish (Processor Framework barrels expose target and current quality).
- Advisor checks for Medieval Overhaul's production chains: hides rotting unprocessed, tanning rack without liquor,
  schematics with no bookcase in the research room, barrels outside 7 to 32 °C, worn-out plowed soil.
- Medieval Total Rim's choice quests as council votes, recorded in the chronicle.
- Messenger table, bird post and Rimedieval's signal fire as encounter sources; the explorer's workbench and its
  cultist hideouts for a "root out the cult" ambition and vendettas against the Cultists.

## Guests, factions and the world (Hospitality, Outposts, Factional War, Diplomatic Marriage, VEE)
- Hospitality: a guest-wing room plan, wishes for better guest rooms, a "renowned hosts" ambition, a council
  dilemma when a happy guest asks to join.
- Outposts: posting a bitter or quarrelsome colonist away by decree or dilemma; "letter from the outpost" journals;
  a fair rule for officers away at election time.
- Diplomatic Marriage: "married into X" instead of "gone"; proposals put to a council vote.
- Factional War and Faction Territories: ride out for allies or bar the gates; vassal tithes as mercy-versus-greed
  decisions; a "hold N vassals" ambition.
- Vanilla Events Expanded: the traitor wanderer as a secret the colony uncovers (and a nemesis if they escape);
  whiteout refugees as a mercy-versus-food dilemma; Rimfolk achievements for Vanilla Achievements Expanded.

## People, magic and beliefs (RimWorld of Magic, VSE, VTE, VIE, races)
- Done: magic classes as tags (mage, fighter, healer, gifted) in offices (physician favors healers, marshal
  fighters), decree reactions, a magic circle wish, dreams, tones and a class-level life goal. Still open:
  necromancy dilemmas, an epithet for magic deeds (RWoM keeps no records to count).
- Merren charm outcomes as a secret or "the song is fading" dilemma.
- Done: Vanilla Skills Expanded expertise as a life goal. Still open: expertise titles ("the Weaponsmith"), Alpha
  Skills' conditional passions as wishes.
- Done: aristocratic, royal and god-emperor memes (VIE Memes) make the leader appoint the council; memes from
  Ideology, VIE Memes and Alpha Memes in decree reactions and the colony's ways.
- Done: Vanilla Traits Expanded traits in decree reactions, offices, the colony's ways, wishes (academian, snob,
  tycoon, wanderlust, neat, animal lover, martial artist) and tones.
- Done: Vanilla Social Interactions Expanded's teaching, venting, memories and best friends feed the social web;
  Rimfolk's own cheering-up and plain lessons step back while it's on.

## Nature and beasts (Alpha Animals, Megafauna, Dinosauria, biomes, weather)
- Alpha Animals' stalking arctic lion as a pre-announced beast nemesis with dread and a dream omen; the big
  creature events as dilemmas or a "great hunt" ambition.
- Landform- and biome-aware flavor (Geological Landforms tile mutators, Biome Transitions): "down in the valley",
  "at the edge of the desert".
- Honey wishes by category; ReGrowth hot springs for the bath wish; planter boxes need daylight in a medieval colony.

## Making things (VE Cooking and Brewing, VFE, Dubs Bad Hygiene, VE Books)
- Bathroom and sauna room plans; a bath wish that prefers bathtub, hot tub or sauna.
- Processor awareness: a stew pot out of wood, a barrel spoiling from heat, legendary wine ready.
- A noble bedroom plan with artisan furniture, a library plan with VE Books' writer's table, a "write my memoir"
  life goal.
- Medieval defense: VFE Security's catapults, ballistas and spikes for a "man the walls" decree or siege dilemma.

## Interface and play (Interaction Bubbles, RimHUD, Melee Animation, Snap Out!, Replace Stuff)
- More Rimfolk moments in the social log, so Interaction Bubbles shows them.
- A RimHUD widget with trust, the open wish, office and life goal.
- Melee Animation duels: the duel spot for the training wish, friendly duels between rivals, a duel win over a
  nemesis settles the score.
- A Snap Out! calm-down as a social moment that builds a bond.
- Replace Stuff: one click to replace the wooden walls the fire warning flags with stone.

## Watch out for (conflicts in the modlist, from the mods' own pages)
- Haul to Stack does nothing while Pick Up And Haul is installed.
- Better Pawn Control lists A RimWorld of Magic as incompatible.
- Giddy-Up 2 lists Allies Are Helpful as reported incompatible.
- Melee Animation officially covers Medieval Overhaul, Outland and RimWorld of Magic weapons, not Medieval Total
  Rim's armory, the al9000 packs or Elves.
